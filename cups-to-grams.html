Cups to grams: conversion chart by ingredient

How many grams are in a cup of flour, sugar or butter? A cup weighs differently for every ingredient: a cup of flour is about 127 g and a cup of sugar about 204 g. Here are cups, tablespoons and teaspoons in grams (and ounces) for 33 common ingredients.

Measures used: 240 ml cup, 15 ml tablespoon and 5 ml teaspoon, ingredient spooned in and not packed. Values are approximate: for precise baking, use a kitchen scale.

How to measure with cups

  • Flour and cocoa: spoon them into the cup and level off with a knife, without packing or tapping. Scooping straight from the bag can add 20% more.
  • Brown sugar: recipes often ask for it "packed", which weighs a little more than the chart.
  • Butter: one stick is ½ cup, about 113 g (4 oz).
  • Liquids: water, milk or juice weigh practically the same in grams as in milliliters.

Cup sizes around the world

The US legal cup is 240 ml and the customary cup 236.6 ml; the UK, Australia and Canada use 250 ml. The difference (1–4%) won't change a home recipe.
