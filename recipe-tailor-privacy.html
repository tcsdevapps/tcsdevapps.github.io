Privacy policy for “Recipe Tailor” (Receta a medida)

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who is responsible

The Android app “Recipe Tailor” (called “Receta a medida” in Spanish) is developed and published by Tcs Dev (owner: Jesús Gómez Martín), based in Tres Cantos (Madrid), Spain. Contact: tcsdev.apps@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There are no accounts or sign-up.
  • Your recipes are stored only on your phone.
  • Recipe photos can be read in two ways, as you choose: with the phone's reader (the photo never leaves the phone) or with AI (the photo is sent encrypted to be read and is not stored). PDFs and text files are always read on the phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use data from your device according to the consent you give.

Recipes you save

The text of your recipes, their servings and your settings are stored in the app's internal storage on your device. We do not receive them and have no access to them. If Google backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include them in that backup, which Google manages under its own privacy policy. You can delete them by removing each recipe or uninstalling the app.

Photos, camera and files

If you choose to read a recipe from a photo, the camera or a file (image, PDF or text), the app only accesses the file you select or share. It does not ask for permission to see your gallery or your files.

With the phone's reader, text recognition happens on the device itself with Google's ML Kit (through Google Play Services). Images are not sent to us or to Google. Photos taken with the camera from the app are stored temporarily and deleted as soon as they have been read. For maintenance purposes, ML Kit may send Google technical diagnostic and usage data (device model, system and app version, performance and errors), without any image content. More information in the ML Kit terms and privacy.

Reading photos with AI (optional)

The first time you read a photo, the app explains this option and you choose whether to use it. You can change it at any time in Settings › Read photos with AI. If you don't use it, none of what is described here happens.

What is sent. The recipe photos you choose to read, reduced and re-saved on the phone before sending, so they carry no GPS location or other hidden data (phone model, date…). Along with them, a random installation identifier is sent, created by the app, which does not identify you (it changes if you reinstall the app), and, as with any internet connection, the phone's IP address. The transfer is encrypted (HTTPS).

To whom and why. The photos reach our server, hosted by Cloudflare, which passes them to Anthropic's AI service (the Claude model) only to transcribe the recipe. The recipe comes back to your phone, where you review it before saving. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and, according to its data retention terms, does not keep it except in limited cases (when required by law or if its safety systems detect possible abuse).

What we keep. Our server does not store the photos or the recipe text. It only keeps a count of readings per installation identifier and per IP address to apply the daily limit, which is deleted every day, and technical logs without content (for example, how many photos a reading had and how long it took), which Cloudflare keeps for a few days.

App check. So that the server only serves the original app installed from Google Play, the app asks Google for a Play Integrity token, which Google generates with information about the app and the integrity of the device, and which the server verifies with Google. More information in the Play Integrity documentation.

Cloudflare and Anthropic act as data processors and may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example, in the United States), with the safeguards provided for by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. More information in the privacy policies of Cloudflare and Anthropic.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure them and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect the advertising ID, the IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), device information and interactions with the ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's messaging platform when you open the app. You can change it at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. More information on how Google uses data in advertising.

Purchase to remove ads

The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; only the confirmation that the purchase exists, so the ads can be turned off.

Ratings

The app may show Google Play's official rating window. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play; the app does not see them.

Legal basis and retention

Recipe data is processed on your device to provide the service you request. Reading photos with AI is based on your choice to use it to read the recipe you ask us to (provision of the service), and the daily limit on the legitimate interest of preventing abuse and keeping the service running. Personalised advertising is based on your consent, and non-personalised advertising on the legitimate interest of funding the free app. We do not keep photos or recipes on our server; only the daily counters and technical logs described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to tcsdev.apps@gmail.com. For data processed by Google AdMob, ML Kit and Play Integrity, you can also contact Google, and for AI reading, Cloudflare and Anthropic. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the date above.
