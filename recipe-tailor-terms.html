What the app is

A tool to save recipes, adapt them to other servings or pans, and convert kitchen measurements. It is free and funded by ads. A one-time in-app purchase removes the ads; it is handled by Google Play and kept on your devices with the same Google account.

Reading photos with AI

  • It is an optional feature: you can also read photos with the phone's reader or type the recipe by hand.
  • For fair use, each device has a limited number of AI readings per day. When the limit is reached, the app uses the phone's reader until the next day. The limit may change to keep the service running.
  • Without the purchase to remove ads, each AI reading is offered in exchange for watching an ad, which you choose to watch.
  • The feature relies on external services and an internet connection. We may modify, limit or suspend it, temporarily or permanently; if so, the app will keep working with the phone's reader.
  • Use AI reading only to read recipes, with photos you have the right to use. Automated use, use outside the app or for other purposes is not allowed.

Always check your recipes

Photo readings (with AI or the phone's reader) and the calculations of quantities and conversions may contain errors. Check quantities, ingredients, temperatures and times before cooking, and pay special attention to allergens and any health or dietary needs. The app is an aid and does not replace your own judgement.
